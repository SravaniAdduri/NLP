Cloud Operations Runbook

Service: Billing API
Environment: production
Primary Region: us-east-1
Secondary Region: us-west-2

SLO Targets
- Availability: 99.95%
- p95 Latency: under 250 ms

Deployment Window
- Tuesdays and Thursdays
- 02:00 to 04:00 UTC

Incident Severity Definitions
- SEV-1: Full outage affecting all customers
- SEV-2: Major degradation affecting multiple customers
- SEV-3: Minor degradation or single-customer impact

Escalation Policy
1. On-call engineer acknowledges alert within 5 minutes.
2. Incident commander assigned within 10 minutes for SEV-1 and SEV-2.
3. Customer communication every 30 minutes during active incident.

Backup and Recovery
- Full database backup every 24 hours.
- Incremental backups every 4 hours.
- Recovery Point Objective (RPO): 15 minutes.
- Recovery Time Objective (RTO): 60 minutes.

Postmortem Requirements
- Draft within 48 hours.
- Final review within 7 days.
- Include timeline, root cause, corrective actions, and owners.
