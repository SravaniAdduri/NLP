# University Policy Handbook (Excerpt)

## Attendance Policy
- Minimum attendance requirement is 75% for each registered course.
- Students below 75% may be barred from end-semester exams unless condoned.

## Examination Rules
- Midterm weightage: 30%
- End-semester weightage: 50%
- Continuous assessment weightage: 20%
- Passing mark for each course: 40 out of 100.

## Project and Thesis Guidelines
- M.Tech thesis carries 18 credits.
- Thesis proposal submission deadline: Week 6 of Semester 3.
- Final thesis defense is evaluated by one internal and one external examiner.

## Grading Scale
- O: 90-100
- A+: 80-89
- A: 70-79
- B+: 60-69
- B: 50-59
- C: 40-49
- F: Below 40

## Academic Integrity
- Plagiarism above 20% similarity requires mandatory review.
- Repeat violations may lead to course failure.
- All submissions must include citation references for external sources.
